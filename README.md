# MimeWalk

Make walking in place look like real walking in VRChat. MimeWalk speeds
you up and slows you down on every step, in time with your mime walking.

- **`app/mimewalk.py`** (`MimeWalk.exe`): a small console app. It reads
  your avatar's menu over OSC and sends a pulsing movement input. Settings are generated
  in `config.toml`.
- **`MimeWalk.unitypackage`**: adds a MimeWalk menu to your radial menu (Walk, Feedback,
  Tempo, Surge, Speed) and an optional light and click on each step to any
  avatar, through Modular Avatar or VRCFury. The light uses Poiyomi Toon and
  shows only in your own view.
